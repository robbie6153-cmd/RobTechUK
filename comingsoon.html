ScoreCast24: Ready for the 2026/27 Season

Following a successful trial during the World Cup, ScoreCast24 is now ready for the 2026/27 football season.

We are currently planning a new promotional campaign designed to introduce ScoreCast24 to more football supporters, workplaces, friendship groups, clubs, and communities across the country.

Players will be able to predict football scores, compete on weekly and season-long leaderboards, and create or join private mini leagues with friends, family, colleagues, and other supporters.

Introducing the Weekly Dream Team

ScoreCast24 is also introducing a new weekly Dream Team competition.

Unlike traditional fantasy football games, you will not be tied to the same squad for an entire season. Instead, you can select a completely new team every week based on the upcoming fixtures, player form, and your own football knowledge.

This gives every player a fresh opportunity to compete each week, even if they missed an earlier round or had a disappointing previous score.

We are hoping to work with sponsors to provide weekly prizes and additional rewards. Our intention is to keep ScoreCast24 completely free to play.

Personalised Websites for Content Creators

RobTechUK is now creating personalised subscription websites for independent content creators.

RobTechUK can build and launch your website without an upfront development fee. In return, RobTechUK receives 10% of the revenue generated through the website.

Whether you are a journalist, filmmaker, YouTuber, podcaster, entertainer, educator, or adult content creator, your own website can give you greater control over your content, audience, pricing, and income.

We can create a personalised website and domain where you can upload videos, offer secure subscriptions, sell individual content, and set your own prices and membership rates.

Your website can also include merchandise sales, exclusive members-only content, and paid personalised video message requests.

Instead of relying entirely on large third-party platforms, creators can build their own independent brand and maintain a direct relationship with their audience.

Built Around Your Brand

Every RobTechUK creator website can be personalised around the creator’s name, branding, audience, and type of content.

The aim is to provide creators with a professional, secure, and easy-to-manage platform without the expense of paying for a large website build upfront.
