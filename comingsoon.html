ScoreCast24 2026/27

Following a successful World Cup trial, ScoreCast24 is now ready for the 2026/27 football season.

We are preparing a new promotional campaign to introduce ScoreCast24 to more football supporters, workplaces, friendship groups, clubs and communities.

Players will be able to compete in weekly score prediction games, season leaderboards and private mini leagues with friends, family and colleagues.

Introducing the Weekly Dream Team

ScoreCast24 is also introducing a brand-new Weekly Dream Team competition.

Unlike traditional fantasy football games, you will not be tied to the same squad for an entire season. Instead, you can select a completely new team every week.

This gives every player a fresh opportunity to compete, even after missing a previous week or recording a disappointing score.

We are hoping to work with sponsors to provide weekly prizes and additional rewards. Our intention is to keep ScoreCast24 completely free to play.

Websites for Content Creators

RobTechUK is now creating personalised websites for independent content creators, complete with secure subscriptions and payment options.

Instead of paying a large upfront development fee, RobTechUK can build and launch your website free of charge in return for 10% of the revenue generated through the platform.

Whether you are a journalist, filmmaker, YouTuber, podcaster or adult content creator looking for greater control over your work, we can create a website built around your own name and brand.

Your personalised domain can allow you to upload videos, create members-only content, set your own subscription rates and charge individual prices for selected content.

Your website can also include merchandise sales and requests for paid personalised video messages through RobTechUK.

The aim is to give creators more independence, greater control over their prices and a direct relationship with their audience without relying entirely on large third-party platforms.

Future Development

RobTechUK is also working towards larger and more ambitious projects, including games based in real neighbourhoods and familiar local areas.

Imagine playing a game set around your own streets, local landmarks and community, or seeing your home area taken over by a zombie apocalypse.

These ideas are still at an early stage, but the long-term aim is to create games that feel more personal, local and different from traditional releases.
